Who we are and what the apps do

The apps are run by Calda d.o.o., Gospodinjska ulica 8, 1000 Ljubljana, Slovenia (registration number 9247688000, tax number SI 21100489) ("Calda", "we"). We are the controller of the limited information described here. Contact: development@thecalda.com.

Calda builds club apps that run inside ChatGPT, for example the Bologna FC 1909 app. When you ask ChatGPT about the club, ChatGPT calls our server and shows our cards: fixtures, match results, standings, squad, tickets, merchandise, directions to the stadium and similar.

You do not create an account with us and you never sign in. The apps only read public sports and club information; they cannot buy anything, post anything or change anything on your behalf.

This policy covers our apps and server only. ChatGPT itself is run by OpenAI under OpenAI's privacy policy.

What information we process

We only receive what ChatGPT sends us to answer a request, plus anonymous clicks inside our cards. We never receive your name, email, ChatGPT account or conversation.

What we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or ChatGPT account details
  • Your conversation with ChatGPT; we only see the single request made to our app
  • Passwords or login details; the apps have no accounts
  • Payment details; purchases happen on the club's or retailer's own website
  • Your precise device location (GPS); we only use a place you type yourself
  • Health, government ID or other sensitive data

We do not sell information, show advertising based on you, or build profiles of individual users.

Retention, security, children and transfers

Retention. Requests and typed places are not stored. Anonymous usage statistics are kept for 13 months and then deleted. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for 7 days.

Security. Data travels over HTTPS. The usage database is locked so the public cannot read it; only Calda staff who run the apps can see the statistics. Secret keys stay on our server and are never sent to your device.

Children. The apps are suitable for a general audience, including teenagers, and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

International transfers. Our providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards they offer, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Hosting regions: server Central EU, database Central EU.

Your rights and choices

You can stop all processing by disconnecting the app in ChatGPT (Settings → Apps). Under the GDPR you may ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to our use of it, or complain to a data protection authority, in Slovenia the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec).

Because we store no names, accounts or contact details, we usually cannot link any record to you. If you send us the date and time you used an app, we will check our logs and help where we can.
